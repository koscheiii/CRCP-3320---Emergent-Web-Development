Do Together

A web app that helps couples, friends, and individuals find fun things to do whether it's together or alone.

What's the move?

About This Project

Do Together started as a simple idea: sometimes deciding what to do is harder than doing it. This project will evolve into a full-stack app that generates activity ideas with mood and budget filters, allowing users to save and share favorites.

About the Creator

Hi! I'm Isabelle, a creative technologist who loves blending design, digital experiences, and everyday life moments. This project is a way for me to explore full-stack development while building something cozy and fun.
